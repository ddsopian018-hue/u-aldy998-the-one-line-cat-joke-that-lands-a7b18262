A one-line cat joke built around a single misdirection: the setup hires the cat as a therapist — paid in treats, napping through every session — and the turn lands when his only professional advice turns out to be the most cat thing imaginable: knock your problems off the table and stare at them on the floor. No tired pun, no explanation after the punchline, just a setup that promises help and a cat who delivers exactly what cats always deliver.
